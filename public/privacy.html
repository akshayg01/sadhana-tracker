Privacy Policy

Sadhana Tracker

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Sadhana Tracker ("the app") helps devotees record their daily spiritual practice (japa rounds, reading, and hearing) and review their progress. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account to create and identify your account.
  • Sadhana records. The practice data you enter — japa rounds, reading sessions, hearing sessions, dates, and personal targets.
  • Role & group. If assigned, your role (devotee, coordinator, or admin) and congregation group, used to show the right features and reports.

How we use your information

  • To record and display your daily sadhana and progress.
  • To let you track streaks, weekly summaries, and targets.
  • To provide congregation reporting to authorized coordinators and administrators of your group only.
  • To secure your account and operate the service.

Who can see your records

Your individual records are private to you. They are visible only to you and to coordinators or administrators who are explicitly authorized for your congregation group. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How your data is stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase (our database and authentication provider) and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access is restricted by row-level security so each user can access only the records they are permitted to see.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated records at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will remove your data within a reasonable period after your request.

Third-party services

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth) authentication.
  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your data.
  • Vercel — hosting of the web application.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for devotees recording their own practice and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
