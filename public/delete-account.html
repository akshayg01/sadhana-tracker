You can request deletion of your Sadhana Tracker account and all data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to akshayg01@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in (so we can verify it is your account).
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and associated data, then confirm by email.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, email, role, and group association)
  • All of your daily sadhana records (japa rounds and dates)
  • Your reading and hearing sessions
  • Your reflections and any personal targets

What is retained, and for how long

Your personal records are deleted and not retained. We process verified deletion requests within 30 days. In limited cases, minimal information may be kept only where required by law; otherwise nothing is retained.

Questions

For anything related to your data or this request, contact akshayg01@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
